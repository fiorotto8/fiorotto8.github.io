Research lines from the CV, kept short here because nobody should need a treasure map to discover that I like gaseous detectors.

CYGNO and CYGNUS optical-readout TPCs

Directional Dark Matter detection with optical-readout gaseous TPCs, from LIME to the 400 liter CYGNO04 demonstrator and toward larger CYGNUS concepts.

X-ray polarimetry with optical TPCs

Triple-GEM optical-readout TPCs for hard X-ray polarimetry, connecting underground detector craft to high-energy astrophysics. Same detector habits, new cosmic gossip.

PICOSEC Micromegas and Muon Collider timing

Fast-timing detector R&D for Muon Collider environments, where nanoseconds are leisurely and picoseconds are the local currency.

CMS GEM detector R&D and operations

Triple-GEM detector development, commissioning, gas systems, test beams, and operations for the CMS muon system.

Simulation, operations, and technical coordination

Geant4 simulations, detector-response modelling, commissioning strategy, technical coordination, and the recurring question of where the latest drawing went.
