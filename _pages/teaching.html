Teaching, supervision, school laboratories, public seminars, visits, and other ways of persuading physics to leave the detector hall and speak human.

Student Supervision

Co-supervisor of 3 GSSI PhD students, supervisor of Bachelor/Master students at Pavia, and supervisor of CERN Summer Student and Short-Term Internship projects. The style: understand the physics, respect the hardware, interrogate the plot.

Laboratory Teaching

Lecturer for the GSSI PhD course "Laboratory of Low-Energy Radiation Measurement" and tutor for hands-on detector schools at LNGS and CERN, including DRD1 and RD51 activities.

Outreach

INFN LNGS guide, former CMS guide, CYGNO outreach/social-media coordinator, public seminar speaker, European Researchers Night contributor, and occasional translator between particle physics and normal human conversation.

Scientific Service

Organizer and laboratory manager for detector schools and collaboration meetings, including CYGNO, DRD1, RD51, Gran Sasso Hands-on PhD Summer School, and Frontiers in X-ray Polarimetry Academy. Also referee for JINST and EPJ Techniques and Instrumentation.
